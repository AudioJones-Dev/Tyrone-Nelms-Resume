# LinkedIn, Maintained as Code

The LinkedIn presence is a **deployment target** of this repository: headline, About section, and every experience entry are drafted, reviewed, and versioned in [`linkedin/`](https://github.com/AudioJones-Dev/Tyrone-Nelms-Resume/tree/main/linkedin) before anything goes live. Edits happen in the repo first — the profile never drifts from the record.

## Current Headline

> Operations & Business Systems Architect | Business Analysis, Requirements Engineering, Systems Implementation, AI-Enabled Workflows

## What Lives in the LinkedIn OS

- **Profile surfaces** — headline options with keyword rationale, prepared About copy, and experience entries using the approved chronology, plus a gated Featured plan and recommendations strategy.
- **Growth surfaces** — creator positioning, five content pillars mapped to the positioning doctrine, reusable post frameworks with hard guardrails (no unverified metrics, no client leakage), and outreach sequences with stop rules.

## The Guardrails

Every post and message passes the same tests as this site: claims trace to the canonical record, unverified numbers stay out, and client-adjacent stories require cleared gates. The full ruleset is public in the [LinkedIn OS README](https://github.com/AudioJones-Dev/Tyrone-Nelms-Resume/blob/main/linkedin/README.md).
